info Study Summary

The purpose of this study is to evaluate the safety and efficacy of pembrolizumab (MK-3475) compared to standard chemotherapy in participants with previously untreated advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The study aims to determine if pembrolizumab extends overall survival and slows disease progression in this specific patient population.

Eligibility Criteria

check_circle Inclusion Criteria
